# Limitations and interpretation

- **Market data:** the $825.03 reference close and $51.883bn market capitalization are from Yahoo Finance as of 2026-10-09. They are secondary market-data observations, not company-reported facts.
- **Share count:** 64.525m is the Q2 2026 weighted-average diluted share count used for EPS. It is not a point-in-time shares-outstanding figure, so it should not be used as a precise market-cap calculation.
- **Forte cash bridge:** argenx announced a ~$2.2bn equity value and later disclosed 19.895m shares tendered at $77, while noting that argenx and affiliates already owned shares. The model therefore uses an **estimated** ~$1.816bn incremental cash consideration rather than subtracting the full headline equity value from June cash. This must be reconciled to the Q3 cash-flow statement and acquired cash once reported.
- **Scenario valuation:** bear/base/bull cases are analyst assumptions, not consensus estimates or company guidance. Exit P/E and operating margin assumptions materially drive the 12-month values.
- **Pipeline valuation:** Sjogren's revenue is explicitly zeroed after UNITY was discontinued. FB102 and other investigational programs are not separately probability-weighted in the core scenario model; they are treated as contingent upside/downside and catalysts.
- **Event study:** the sample is small, several events coincide with earnings or other announcements, and the 2026-10-08 UNITY event is confounded by positive FB102 celiac data released the same morning. The event study is descriptive, not causal.
- **Regional data:** the regional revenue split is not current through Q2 2026, so it is not used as a primary investment conclusion.
- **No investment advice:** this repository is an educational interview project based on public information and simplified modeling.
