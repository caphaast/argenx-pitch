# argenx (ARGX) investment memo

**As of:** 9 October 2026  
**Reference price:** $825.03  
**View:** **LONG**  
**12-month target:** **$1,006 (+22%)**  
**Next checkpoint:** Q3 2026 results on 22 October 2026.

## Executive summary

argenx is a high-quality commercial-stage immunology company built around the VYVGART franchise. Product net sales reached $1.516bn in Q2 2026, up 60% year over year and 17% quarter over quarter, while operating profit reached $494m. H1 2026 product sales were $2.813bn and cash plus current financial assets were $5.2bn at 30 June 2026. [1]

The 8 October clinical update reset expectations but did not break the core earnings thesis. The Phase 3 UNITY study in Sjogren's disease was stopped for futility, so the revised valuation assigns zero explicit Sjogren's revenue. The same morning, FB102 met the Phase 2 celiac disease primary endpoint and argenx said it plans to advance the program into Phase 3. [2][3]

My 12-month scenario valuation gives approximately **$479 in the bear case, $1,006 in the base case, and $1,441 in the bull case** versus the $825.03 reference price. The base case assumes 25% FY2027 sales growth, a 38% operating margin, and a 25x exit P/E, producing FY2027E diluted EPS of about $40.23. That growth assumption is a significant deceleration from the latest reported +60% year-over-year Q2 growth and does not require explicit revenue from Sjogren's, FB102, or empasiprubart.

## Investment thesis

**1. The October selloff over-weighted one failed expansion indication relative to the existing franchise.** UNITY removes a meaningful future indication, but it does not directly impair approved VYVGART demand. The investment case is primarily about the durability of the commercial franchise rather than recovering value from Sjogren's.

**2. Commercial growth remains strong enough to support a premium earnings multiple.** Quarterly product net sales increased from roughly $398m in Q1 2024 to $1.516bn in Q2 2026. Q2 reaccelerated 17% sequentially, while operating profit reached $494m. [1] My base case deliberately slows FY2027 growth to 25% while allowing operating margin to expand to 38%.

**3. The long does not require heroic pipeline assumptions.** Sjogren's contributes zero explicit revenue. FB102 and empasiprubart are treated as optionality rather than discrete revenue lines in the core valuation. Positive FB102 Phase 2 celiac results and upcoming MMN data therefore create upside to the base case rather than being necessary to justify the target. [3]

**4. The current price embeds a lower earnings multiple than the target case.** At $825.03, ARGX trades at approximately 20.5x the revised base FY2027 EPS. A 25x multiple yields the $1,006 target. I view that re-rating as defensible if VYVGART sustains strong growth and operating leverage continues to improve.

## What the market may be mispricing

A simple “clinical failure = franchise impairment” interpretation is too broad. The more relevant question is whether the market has discounted the loss of Sjogren's more heavily than the change in long-term earnings power warrants.

A reverse-valuation check helps frame the risk. Under a 25x 2030 exit P/E, 40% operating margin, and a 10% required annual return, the current price requires roughly **$8.9bn of 2030 product sales**, or approximately **9.4% annualized growth from the model's FY2026E base**. That hurdle is materially below current reported growth and provides a useful test of whether the valuation is truly demanding.

## Pipeline reset after 8 October

**UNITY / Sjogren's:** argenx discontinued the Phase 3 UNITY study after a futility recommendation from the independent monitoring committee. No new safety signal was identified. [2] The revised model assigns zero Sjogren's revenue.

**FB102 / celiac disease:** FB102 met its Phase 2 primary endpoint for change in villus-height-to-crypt-depth ratio (p=0.0176), and argenx plans to advance it into Phase 3 development. [3] I treat this as optionality rather than core modeled revenue.

**Other catalysts:** positive ALKIVIA Phase 3 data in myositis support further efgartigimod expansion, while the EMPASSION MMN readout for empasiprubart is expected in Q4 2026. These are meaningful upside paths but also sources of binary risk.

## Forte acquisition and balance-sheet treatment

argenx agreed to acquire Forte for $77 per share in cash, representing approximately $2.2bn of total equity value, and completed the transaction on 27 August 2026. The completion release states that 19.895m shares were tendered and that those shares, together with shares already owned by argenx and affiliates, represented about 87.13% of Forte's outstanding shares. [4][5]

The $2.2bn headline equity value should not simply be subtracted from the $5.2bn June cash balance as incremental post-quarter cash paid. The model uses an estimated incremental consideration of about **$1.82bn**, implying illustrative pro forma cash of about **$3.38bn before acquired cash, option treatment, fees, and other closing adjustments**. This remains an analyst estimate pending Q3 reconciliation.

## Catalysts and thesis breakers

**Near-term catalysts:** Q3 sales at or above the modeled trajectory, continued operating leverage, successful EMPASSION/MMN data, and continued uptake across VYVGART indications.

**Thesis breakers:** material VYVGART deceleration, margin deterioration despite scale, repeated late-stage clinical failures that undermine the premium multiple, or acquisition-related cash use materially worse than the current bridge.

The key near-term checkpoint is Q3 results on 22 October. I would focus on product sales growth, operating expense discipline, post-Forte cash, and any changes to the catalyst calendar.

## Investment conclusion

**LONG ARGX at $825.03 with a 12-month target of $1,006, or approximately 22% upside.** The thesis is that the October reset removed one meaningful but non-core expansion opportunity while leaving the VYVGART earnings engine intact. My base case already assumes a sharp slowdown to 25% FY2027 growth, assigns zero value to Sjogren's, and does not require explicit revenue from FB102 or empasiprubart. If commercial execution remains strong, I believe a 25x FY2027 earnings multiple is justified.

I would reassess the long if VYVGART growth or operating leverage materially breaks. The point of the pitch is not that the clinical downside disappeared; it is that the current price offers an attractive entry into a high-growth commercial franchise after expectations were reset.
